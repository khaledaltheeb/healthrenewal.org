# Rawafid Self-Tests — Arabic Item Writing Standard

Status: mandatory for every interactive test.

## Core rule
Every question must be immediately understandable to an ordinary Arabic reader. Scientific precision belongs in the construct specification and interpretation; item wording must remain plain, direct and natural.

## Mandatory rules
- Use Modern Standard Arabic that feels natural and familiar.
- Prefer one short sentence per item.
- Ask about one idea only.
- Avoid technical psychological terminology inside items unless absolutely unavoidable.
- Avoid double negatives.
- Avoid two questions joined with «و» when they can produce different answers.
- Avoid vague words such as «غالبًا» when a defined frequency scale already supplies the frequency.
- Use a clear reference period when needed: «خلال الأسبوعين الماضيين…» or «في العادة…».
- Avoid moral judgment, blame, stigma, and language that suggests a diagnosis.
- Avoid leading wording that signals the “good” answer.
- Avoid unnecessarily abstract metaphors.
- Keep answer options stable and predictable across a section.
- If a reverse-keyed item is required, make it simple rather than linguistically tricky.

## Readability check
Before publication, every item must pass these questions:
1. Can the user understand it on the first read?
2. Does it ask only one thing?
3. Is the timeframe clear?
4. Could two reasonable readers interpret it differently?
5. Is there a simpler wording with the same meaning?
6. Does any word require psychological training to understand?

If #4 or #6 is yes, rewrite.

## Examples
Bad: «أجد نفسي منخرطًا في اجترار معرفي متكرر ذي محتوى سلبي.»
Good: «أعود إلى الفكرة المزعجة نفسها مرارًا، حتى عندما لا أصل إلى حل.»

Bad: «لا أشعر أنني غير قادر على إيقاف التفكير.»
Good: «أجد صعوبة في إيقاف التفكير عندما يبدأ.»

Bad: «أفكر في أخطائي وأشعر بالذنب تجاهها.»
Good: split into two items if both constructs matter.

## User experience
The interface may show a short optional clarification under an item, but the question itself must remain understandable without that clarification.
